# Substrate

The worldview the system rests on. Internal design document. Phase 4 (writing the 64 hexagram entries and 8 channel-state entries) draws from this directly; if you can't derive an entry from this substrate, the substrate has a gap.

## 1. What this system is for

The system offers a fresh description of the consulter's situation. When habitual thinking has stalled, an unfamiliar framing of the moment can open a path that wasn't visible before. The primary offer is attitude adjustment - not through instruction, but through the image itself. The consulter sits with the description and applies it; the system does not direct them.

Two quieter functions run alongside the surface offer:
- *Transformation*: sustained engagement with the symbols does cumulative work over time, even when the immediate use is reframing.
- *Character building*: repeated use shapes who the consulter is becoming, even though no single reading names that explicitly.

Neither is the stated goal; both are real.

What this rules out at the structural level: direct prediction, prescriptive instruction, coaching voice, any framing that positions the system as authoritative about the consulter's specific situation.

## 2. Channels and their properties

The anchor concept is the **channel**. A moment in a life is a configuration of channels - to other people, to your own interior, to your work, to circumstances, to ideas. You're always in many channels; at any moment, only some of them are live, and they're configured in particular ways. The shape of *which channels are live and how they're configured* is your situation.

Each channel can be characterized by three independent binary properties:

- **Open or closed** — is the channel carrying anything right now, or sitting quiet?
- **Focused or distributed** — is it point-to-point (one connection, real depth) or spread across many?
- **Outward or inward** — is energy flowing out from you, or coming in toward you?

The two values along each axis are the system's primary binary, named **on / off** at the line level (on = the property holds: open, focused, outward; off = it doesn't: closed, distributed, inward). On/off is a deliberate choice for plainness and electronics-foundational legibility: anyone can read it, and the multiple interpretive frames (signal/silence, charge/ground, present/absent, etc.) can be layered into entry writing without committing to any single frame.

What on/off is NOT: not yang/yin (the gendered cosmology of the original is rejected), not good/bad (both are necessary; neither is preferred), not full/empty in a moral sense. On just means present; off is at rest, latent, holding space.

## 3. The eight channel states

The three binary properties combine into 2³ = 8 distinct channel states. Each is a particular posture a channel can be in.

Convention: lines read bottom-to-top.
- Bottom line: open (1) or closed (0)
- Middle line: focused (1) or distributed (0)
- Top line: outward (1) or inward (0)

| # | Pattern (b-m-t) | Name | Posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | open / focused / outward | **POUR** | Concentrated outward energy through one channel. Devoted work, sustained giving, focused offering. |
| 2 | open / focused / inward | **DRAW** | Concentrated reception from one source. Deep listening, careful watching, sustained attention received. |
| 3 | open / distributed / outward | **BROADCAST** | Energy spread outward across many. Publication, performance, scattered seed. |
| 4 | open / distributed / inward | **GATHER** | Energy taken in widely from many. Scanning, reading the room, sampling broadly. |
| 5 | closed / focused / outward | **AIM** | Held intention pointed at one target, not yet active. A vow not yet enacted, the nocked arrow. |
| 6 | closed / focused / inward | **WATCH** | Watchful attention on one source, awaiting return. Vigil, expectant attention. |
| 7 | closed / distributed / outward | **STAND** | Wide reach available but currently silent. Reserved capacity, an empty stage. |
| 8 | closed / distributed / inward | **ROOM** | Wide availability open to many but currently empty. Hospitality without guests, a hollow waiting to be filled. |

Each channel state has a fuller per-state description (range across domains, examples in inner life / relationships / work / body / world) - see entry templates and the planned trigram entries.

The four "active" states (POUR, DRAW, BROADCAST, GATHER) pair with their "latent" counterparts (AIM, WATCH, STAND, ROOM). Active and latent share two properties (focus and direction) and differ on open/closed: the active state is carrying energy; the latent state is the same posture held but not currently flowing. This active/latent pairing is the most semantically useful relation among the 8 in this worldview.

## 4. Composition

A trigram represents one channel, fully described by the three properties stacked. A hexagram represents **two channels in temporal relation**:

- **Lower trigram**: the channel that is live *right now*. The most active channel in the consulter's situation at the moment of casting.
- **Upper trigram**: the channel that is *emerging*. The channel coming into being - whether through the consulter's action, through circumstance, or through what's about to claim attention.

The two channels needn't be the same channel evolving; they can be two different channels (one fading as another rises). The relationship between them is what each hexagram entry articulates.

The 64 hexagrams are 64 distinct trajectories from a present channel state to an emerging channel state.

The upper/lower distinction is therefore *temporal* (now / becoming), not *spatial* (not inner/outer, not self/other). This is a deliberate departure from the original I Ching's outer-trigram-meets-inner-trigram framing.

## 5. Change

Change is built into the hexagram structure itself. Every reading is a transition - present channel → emerging channel. There is no separate "changing lines" mechanism.

This is a deliberate simplification away from the I Ching's two-hexagram-via-changing-lines architecture. Justification: once the hexagram itself encodes a transition, changing lines do redundant work. A reading shows the trajectory directly; nothing further is needed to convey movement.

Practical consequence for the consulter: every casting yields one hexagram, and every hexagram is read as a movement (not a static state). There is no "second hexagram" to consult; no per-line readings to surface conditionally; no special interpretation of stable-vs-changing lines.

## 6. Casting

Six binary draws, producing a 6-bit hexagram. Lines read bottom-to-top: line 1 (first drawn) is the foundation of the lower trigram; line 4 is the foundation of the upper trigram.

Any randomization that produces six bits works:
- One coin flipped six times (heads = on, tails = off)
- A single die per line (even = on, odd = off)
- In the web app, a single click that generates 6 random bits
- An entered seed string (allows reproducible / shareable readings)

The 3-coin or yarrow-stalk methods of the original I Ching are not used; they exist to produce four-outcomes-per-line, which is unnecessary without changing lines.

Framing of the act: the consulter holds a question or sits with a situation, then draws. The question can be specific or open. The system doesn't prescribe a particular ritual register; the consulter brings whatever feels right.

## 7. Stance toward divination

Take the casting seriously enough to make it work. Don't require metaphysical commitment to the casting being meaningful in any cosmic sense.

The reading lands as useful because the consulter brings attention to it. The system gives you a structured frame for your situation that you might not have generated on your own; the work happens when you sit with that frame and notice what it shows. Whether the casting "really" picked the matching hexagram (some kind of synchronicity) or whether you're finding resonance with whichever hexagram you got (psychological pareidolia) is beside the point - the reframing happens regardless.

This is the chaos-magic both/and: a consulter who reads the system as "random prompt + framework for thinking" gets value from it. A consulter who reads it as "the casting brought me what I needed" gets value too. The system is built so neither stance breaks it.

Practical implication for entry writing: do not appeal to any cosmic mechanism in the entry text. Don't say "this hexagram came to you because..." or "the universe is showing you that..." The entry describes the channel-shift the hexagram represents and lets the consulter map it to their situation themselves. Faith in the casting is the consulter's, not the system's.

## 8. Style guide

Conventions for writing the 64 hexagram entries and 8 channel-state entries in Phase 4.

**Voice: thoughtful friend.** Plain, present-tense language. Not a scholar, not an oracle, not a coach. Someone who has spent real time with the system and is telling the consulter what they see. No academic hedging, no mystical pronouncement, no coaching imperatives.

**Image-grounded.** Every entry centers on a concrete situation or image, not an abstract claim. If the entry doesn't have a concrete picture in it, redraft.

**No jargon.** None of: systems-theory terms (attractor, feedback, entropy), mystical terms (synchronicity, archetype, vibration), academic hedges, AI-jargon. The substrate may quietly draw from these; the surface speaks plainly.

**Coherence with substrate.** Every entry is derivable from this document. The 8 channel states' meanings show up consistently across the 64 hexagrams. A reader who hasn't read the substrate can still use the entries cold; a reader who has sees how each entry expresses the same underlying logic.

**Approachable cold.** No required background - no I Ching knowledge, no chaos-magic familiarity, no philosophical frame. Someone landing on a single hexagram entry from a search result should be able to use it.
