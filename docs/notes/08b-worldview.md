# Phase 3 Task 3 (Revised): Worldview

The substantive worldview the system rests on. Settled through collaborative design. All subsequent tasks (force descriptions, casting, stance, compile) derive from this.

## What this system believes

A moment in a life is a configuration of **channels**. You're always in many channels - to other people, to your own interior, to your work, to circumstances, to ideas, to the world - and at any moment, only some of them are live. The shape of *which channels are live and how they're configured* is your situation.

Channels can be characterized along three independent binary properties:

- **Open or closed** - is the channel carrying anything right now, or sitting quiet?
- **Focused or distributed** - is it point-to-point (one connection, real depth) or spread across many?
- **Outward or inward** - is energy flowing out from you, or coming in toward you?

These three properties describe a channel completely for divinatory purposes. The 8 possible combinations are 8 distinct channel postures - 8 ways a channel can be configured in a moment.

## What each layer represents

**A line** is one binary property of one channel. On = the property holds (open, focused, outward); off = it doesn't (closed, distributed, inward).

**A trigram** is one channel, fully described by all three properties stacked:
- Bottom line: open / closed (the foundation - is anything happening here?)
- Middle line: focused / distributed (the body - what shape is the connection?)
- Top line: outward / inward (the surface - which way is energy moving?)

The 8 possible trigram patterns are the 8 distinct channel postures. (Detailed descriptions in `09-channel-states.md`.)

**A hexagram** is two channels in temporal relation: a present channel (lower trigram) and an emerging channel (upper trigram). The 64 hexagrams are 64 distinct trajectories from "what's happening now" to "what's coming into being."

The two channels needn't be the same channel evolving - they can be two different channels (one fading as another rises). The relationship between them is what the entry text articulates.

## What upper/lower means

**Lower trigram = the channel that is live right now.** The most active channel in your situation at the moment of casting.

**Upper trigram = the channel that is emerging.** The channel coming into being, whether through your action, through circumstance, or through what's about to claim attention next.

A reading is: *"Here's the channel you're in right now. Here's the channel about to take over."*

The upper/lower distinction is therefore *temporal*, not *spatial* (not inner/outer, not self/other). This is a deliberate departure from the original I Ching's outer-trigram-meets-inner-trigram framing.

## How change works

Change is built into the hexagram structure itself. Every reading is a transition - present channel → emerging channel. There is no separate "changing lines" mechanism. The temporal information is in the hexagram, not in marked-as-volatile bits.

This is a deliberate simplification away from the I Ching's two-hexagram-via-changing-lines architecture. Justification: once the hexagram itself encodes a transition, changing lines do redundant work.

## What the reading does for the consulter

A reading makes the channel-shift currently happening in your situation visible. By naming the present channel (its three properties) and the emerging channel (its three properties), the system gives you a way to see what's moving in your life that you might not have seen on your own.

This is reframing-as-mechanism: not predicting outcomes, not prescribing actions, just naming the shape of where you are and where you're going so you can sit with it. What you do with the reading is yours.

## What this worldview is NOT

- Not "configurations of forces" (forces is too dynamic-coded; channels is a stable noun for what gets characterized)
- Not "patterns of attention" (attention is too internal; channels include outer-world connections)
- Not "states in a state machine" (too computational; channels are lived, not abstract)
- Not "energies and influences" (too occult; channels is precise without being mystical)

The worldview's anchor metaphor is **the channel**, drawn from both computer-binary (a network channel, a pipe, a stream) and chaos-magic (a connection between magician and target, a current of practice). The dual heritage is intentional: it sits at the intersection of tech and magic without committing to either as ground.
