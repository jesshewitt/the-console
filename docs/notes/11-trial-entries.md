## Trial trigram entry: TUNE

# TUNE (☵)

**Pattern.** closed / focused / inward (bottom: closed, middle: focused, top: inward)

**Core sense.** Tuned to a specific source, no signal arriving yet.

**Description.** A channel that exists - tuned to a specific frequency, ready to receive - but isn't carrying anything right now. The work isn't sending and isn't taking in; the work is the tuning itself. You're holding the channel open in one direction, attentive, expectant. Something will arrive, and you're configured to catch it when it does.

This is the posture of vigil. It isn't passive in the way pure rest is - a closed, distributed, inward channel (MONITOR) is generally available without object; TUNE has a specific object. There's a *where* the attention is pointed, even if no signal is currently flowing.

**Range.**
- *Inner life*: waiting for an answer to surface in your own thinking
- *Relationships*: waiting for a response from someone specific
- *Work*: awaiting feedback on submitted work; the result of a long-running process
- *Body*: holding still, attentive to a specific sensation about to arrive
- *World*: watching for one event to happen (an announcement, a development)

**In combination.** When TUNE meets another channel state, it brings expectant readiness to the meeting. Whatever the other state is doing, this one is waiting for what it points at.

## Trial hexagram entry: TUNE → TRANSMIT

(lower trigram TUNE, upper trigram TRANSMIT; the present channel is TUNE, the emerging channel is TRANSMIT)

# Hexagram XX (☰ over ☵)

**Composition.**
- *Lower (present)*: TUNE (closed / focused / inward)
- *Upper (emerging)*: TRANSMIT (open / focused / outward)

**Core sense.** Tuned waiting becoming concentrated transmission.

**Image / situation.** You've been tuned to one specific thing - listening for an answer to surface, waiting for a particular message, configured to respond when a certain signal arrives. The waiting hasn't been idle; it's been a posture, a held readiness. Now the waiting is ending. What you've been listening for is arriving, or the time to wait passes; either way, the held attention finds its target and the channel opens, transmitting outward in concentrated action. The vigil resolves into the signal it was prepared for.

## What worked, what didn't

**What worked**:
- The substrate's channel-state descriptions gave full bones for the trigram entry (name, pattern, core sense, description, range, in-combination). I didn't invent anything not derivable from substrate Section 3.
- The temporal hexagram structure (present → emerging) made the hexagram entry compose naturally. The "trajectory" framing is doing real work; I could see what kind of entry to write because the worldview said "this is a movement from channel A to channel B."
- Voice came out right: present-tense, image-grounded, no jargon, no prediction. The style guide section was sufficient guidance.
- The signal/communications register holds together when the channel-state names cohere - the entry text naturally reaches for "tuned," "signal," "transmitting" without forcing it.

**What was harder**:
- The hexagram entry needed me to articulate *why* TUNE might become TRANSMIT (the trajectory's narrative logic). The substrate doesn't say "TUNE naturally precedes TRANSMIT" or anything like that - I had to find a coherent reading for this particular pair. This is OK as a creative writing task per entry, but it does mean the entry writer is doing real work *per hexagram* to find the trajectory's logic. Not a substrate gap exactly, but a reminder that 64 entries × this much per-entry thought is real Phase 4 work.

**Substrate gap surfaced**: hexagram numbering convention. The substrate didn't originally specify how hexagrams are numbered. Patched in substrate.md Section 4 with a brief numbering note.
