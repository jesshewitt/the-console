# Existing Landscape: Modern I Ching Reinterpretations

Research conducted 2026-04-25 to inform design of a new modern divination system based loosely on the I Ching.

## 1. Modern/contemporary I Ching books (post-1980)

The "modern" English-language landscape splits roughly into **scholarly translations**, **psychological/Jungian reinterpretations**, and **practical-divination guides**.

- **Stephen Karcher** (with Rudolf Ritsema, *I Ching: The Classic Chinese Oracle of Change*, 1994; solo *Total I Ching: Myths for Change*, 2003; *How to Use the I Ching*, 1997). Came out of the Eranos Foundation's I Ching Project. Mythopoetic, Jungian, deliberately "shatters the constricting cage of traditional interpretations" by mining the Bronze Age divinatory layer and treating each hexagram as a living myth. Influential among Western divinatory readers; voice is poetic, imagistic, present-tense.
- **Jack Balkin**, *The Laws of Change: I Ching and the Philosophy of Life* (2002, rev. 2009). Yale law professor. Logical, ethical, decision-theoretic; explicit reframing of the I Ching as a philosophy-of-life manual rather than a magical tool. Widely cited as the most rigorous contemporary commentary in English.
- **Hilary Barrett**, *I Ching: Walking Your Path, Creating Your Future* (Arcturus, 2010, multiple reissues through 2021), plus the *I Ching with Clarity* community site. Translation plus practical divination guidance; preserves original structure, voice is gentle/coaching.
- **Bradford Hatcher**, *The Book of Changes: Yijing, Word by Word* (2009, 2-vol., free PDF). Scholarly, character-by-character; Hatcher (d. 2020) is the go-to for people who want the lexical bones exposed.
- **Liu Ming**, *Changing: Zhouyi - The Heart of the Yijing* (2005/2016). American Daoist teacher (Da Yuan Circle); strips the Confucian Ten Wings, presents the older Zhouyi layer in a Daoist-practitioner key.
- **Carol K. Anthony** (with Hanna Moog), *I Ching: The Oracle of the Cosmic Way* (2002) and earlier *A Guide to the I Ching* (1979). Inner-work / quasi-therapeutic voice; long-running series.
- **R. L. Wing**, *The I Ching Workbook* (1979). Pioneered the "fill-in your own readings" workbook format - Westernised, pragmatic, business-oriented.
- **Greg Whincup**, *Rediscovering the I Ching* (1986). Mainstream-accessible scholarship.
- **Adele Aldridge**, *I Ching Meditations - A Woman's Book of Changes*. Painting + reflection, gendered re-reading.

## 2. I Ching apps and digital implementations

- **Paul O'Brien's Visionary I Ching** (divination.com, iOS/Android since 2013, lineage back to a 1989 "Synchronicity" CD-ROM). Explicitly "non-sexist, non-militarist" rewrite, art by Joan Larimore. The longest-running commercial digital I Ching.
- **I Ching with Clarity** (onlineclarity.co.uk) - reading tool plus journaling community around Barrett's text.
- **Eclectic Energies, iChingOnline.net, Cafe au Soul, Mothering Change, iching-online.com, yellowbridge.com** - the standard reference/casting genre: pick a method, get a hexagram, read traditional commentary. Visually conservative.
- **AI-Ching / "I Ching AI" / "I Ching GPT" / ichinggpt.com**. Wave of 2023-2025 apps wrapping ChatGPT/GPT-5/DeepSeek over a traditional hexagram engine - "describe your situation, get a matching hexagram + LLM commentary." Generic in voice; no structural reinterpretation.
- **ejfox/cli-ching** (github) - terminal app, local LLM for interpretation. Notable as a hacker-aesthetic counterpoint.
- **Russell Cottrell's Virtual Yarrow Stalks** - faithful simulation of the yarrow-stalk method.
- **József Drasny's Yi-globe** and **Andreas Schöter's Yijing Algebra** - structural visualisations (3-D arrangement; Boolean algebra of lines).
- **J M Berger, Deciphering Wen** - mandalas/animations exploring the King Wen sequence.

## 3. Structural reinterpretations (architecture preserved, meaning rebuilt)

- **James DeKorne, *The Gnostic Book of Changes*** (1980s-90s, full text at jamesdekorne.com). Hexagrams reread through Jungian individuation + Hermetic Kabbalah; treats each hexagram as a stage in psycho-spiritual transformation. The clearest precedent for what's being proposed - and notably eclectic/syncretic rather than single-framework, though still committed to a broadly esoteric-Jungian frame.
- **Stephen Karcher** (above) - structural reinterpretation through myth/archetype rather than psychology.
- **Carol Anthony / Hanna Moog** - structural reread through a quasi-therapeutic "Cosmic Way" cosmology, but committed to a single grounding doctrine.
- **R. L. Wing's Workbook** - lighter-touch structural Westernisation.
- **The Visionary I Ching** (O'Brien) - explicitly de-patriarchalised, de-militarised rewrite of the imagery while preserving structure.
- **Andreas Schöter's Yijing Algebra** - structural reinterpretation of a different kind: re-derives meaning from formal line-algebra.

There is a clear *gap*: no widely known reinterpretation that uses **multiple** contemporary lenses (systems theory + information theory + ecology + queer/feminist + cybernetics) without committing to one as ground truth.

## 4. Generative/computational takes

- **Terence McKenna's Timewave Zero / *The Invisible Landscape*** (1975/1993). Treated the King Wen sequence as an information-theoretic time-fractal; mapped to DNA codons. The canonical "I Ching as code" gesture. Software descendants: Timewave Synthesizer, iancgbell vortex analyses.
- **I-Ching Divination Evolutionary Algorithm (IDEA)** and a 2025 ACM paper *Speculative AI Re-enactment of the Figurists' Encounters with the I Ching* - AI text-embeddings mapping hexagrams to Biblical characters, hand-drawn generative diagrams.
- **Body Oracle** (ACM TEI 2025) - GAN-generated oracle-bone-style characters as a corporeal divination system; closest contemporary "new oracle from scratch."
- **Zora Che, "Oracles in the Machine"** (kernelmag.io) - critical essay framing recommendation systems and astrology apps as opaque algorithmic divination.
- **Quantitative-research papers** on Yijing (Preprints.org 2023; ResearchGate IDEA paper) - billion-hexagram statistical analyses, fractal/symmetry findings.

## 5. Chaos-magic-adjacent / DIY divination

- **Brian Eno & Peter Schmidt, *Oblique Strategies*** (1975, 2001 reissue). Bones-keeping ancestor: deck of aphorisms drawn at impasses. Voice is terse, imperative.
- **Peter J. Carroll, *Liber Null*** (1978) and **Phil Hine, *Condensed Chaos*** (1995). Chaos-magic ethos of building bespoke systems and rotating paradigms (Carroll's "dice of worldviews"). No canonical chaos-magic I Ching reinterpretation exists - which is itself a gap.
- **Michael Bertiaux, *Voudon Gnostic Workbook*** (1988, Weiser reissue 2007). Syncretic, idiosyncratic system-building - the maximalist counter-example to the minimalist Eno deck.
- **Modern oracle-deck wave (2018-2025)**: BOTANICA (language of flowers), Sasuraibito Tarot, plus DIY guides like **Steven Bright's *The Oracle Creator***. Mostly tarot-flavoured; rarely engage with the I Ching's combinatorial structure.
- **William S. Burroughs's cut-ups** - cited as a divination practice in chaos-magic literature; methodological ancestor to generative oracles.

## Gaps in the space

1. **No eclectic, multi-framework structural reinterpretation.** DeKorne is the only well-known fully structural reinterpretation, and he commits to Jung+Kabbalah. There is room for a chaos-magic-style refusal of a single grounding lens.
2. **Apps are either reverent reference or LLM-wrappers.** The 2023-25 AI wave grafts GPT onto traditional commentary. Nobody has shipped an app whose *content* is itself a reinterpretation - the form is the form, the meaning is rewritten.
3. **Visual/interaction language is conservative.** Almost every digital I Ching reproduces the look of a Wilhelm-Baynes paperback. Visionary I Ching is the rare exception, and even it is mild.
4. **No widely-adopted bones-keeping reconstruction.** The closest is Karcher's myth-layer work, but it is still committed to translation. A reading-as-design-fiction/reading-as-poetry artifact (in the spirit of Oblique Strategies but with the I Ching's combinatorial machinery) is unoccupied territory.
5. **Trigram-pair semantics are under-explored as a redesign axis.** Schöter and Drasny attack structure mathematically; nobody seems to have rebuilt the *meaning* of trigram pairs from a contemporary syncretic vocabulary.
6. **Changing-lines mechanic is almost universally treated as inherited rather than reinterpreted.** Open ground.
